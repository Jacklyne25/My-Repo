DEPARTMENTAL SCHEDULING AND ATTENDANCE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (DSAMS)
1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 Purpose
This document specifies the functional and non-functional requirements for the Departmental Scheduling and Attendance Management System (DSAMS). The system is designed to support Heads of Department (HODs), Class Coordinators, and Lecturers in managing timetables, monitoring lecture delivery, recording student attendance, generating analytics, and improving academic operations at departmental level.
1.2 Scope
DSAMS will operate within a single academic department and will provide:
Timetable creation and updates
Lecture session tracking
Attendance recording and verification
Automated analytics and alerts
Reporting dashboards
Role-based access control
1.3 Users
System Administrator
Head of Department (HOD)
Class Coordinator
Lecturer
Student (view-only)

7. ACADEMIC STRUCTURE 
AND
 DATA ENTITIES
7.1 Academic Structure Clarification
Department
: Main academic unit (e.g., Department of Computing and Technology)
Course (Program)
: A specific academic program under one department (e.g., BSIT, DIT)
Each course belongs to only one department
Course Unit (Subject/Module)
: A teaching unit offered within courses (e.g., Database Systems, Networking Fundamentals)
A course unit may be shared across multiple courses
A course unit may also be shared across different departments
This structure enables flexible curriculum design while maintaining departmental ownership of academic programs.
